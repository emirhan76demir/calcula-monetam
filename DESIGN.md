# Design System: Calcula Monetam

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Ledger, Five Cabinets"**

The expression is the interface. One shared shell (a settings rail, a line-by-line editor with right-aligned results, a result column) carries every theme; the shell, layout, controls and component structure never change. Five token themes swap only type, palette, density and one signature move each, selected with `data-theme` on `<html>`. This is a user decision: no single world was chosen, so all directions ship as switchable themes over one build.

- **Moneta** (default, light): coin cabinet. Felt-green rail, cool card stock, gold reserved for the active line and the net. Currency words and unit chips are flat minted metal pills.
- **Tabula** (dark): exchange board. Matte black, amber numerals everywhere a figure appears, red for sell, monospaced caps labels, dashed ledger rules.
- **Spectra** (dark): emission rail. Banded charcoal, bone text, emission-blue sources and a sodium-yellow median on an enlarged source rail.
- **Tessera** (light): ticket coupon. Navy rail, white coupon stock, carbon-purple figures, caps heads, a dashed-perforation breakdown ending in a rotated stamp.
- **Pura** (light): category standard. White, ink, blue, system UI, a bordered white rail.

Density is operational: small type, 1px hairlines, tabular numerals, no cards. Depth is tonal only; the autocomplete popover is the one surface that floats.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One shell, five token themes; themes never change layout or structure.
- Every rule is a 1px hairline; no cards, no shadows as structure.
- Tabular numerals on everything (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on body and editor).
- One reserved accent per theme, spent on the active line, the net and the median. The user's own rates are marked in ink, never accent, so the median stays the rail's only accent mark.
- Signature composition strip and source rail with median appear in every theme.

## Colors

Each theme fills the same role set (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--line-strong`, `--rail*`, `--accent`, `--accent-ink`, `--on-accent`, `--band`, `--sel`, `--focus`, `--pos`, `--neg`, `--chart`, `--var`, `--gold`, `--silver`, `--s1..--s5`, and `--pop` for the two dark themes). The frontmatter lists every color the stylesheet ships, grouped by theme; the per-theme role map lives in the sidecar.

### Primary
- **Cabinet Felt** (cabinet-felt): the rail and mobile top bar; also the 30-day chart line in Moneta.
- **Reserve Gold** (reserve-gold): active segmented option, focus ring, median stroke, wordmark coin, own-rates count badge. Fill and stroke only; as text it becomes **Gold Ink** (gold-ink): active line value, net figure, median scale label, caret, own-rate line and row, selected autocomplete code.

### Secondary
- **Verdigris** (verdigris): variable names in the editor and first composition series.
- **Copper, Slate Blue, Olive Brass, Plum** (copper, slate-blue, olive-brass, plum): composition strip series 2 to 5. **Coin Silver** (coin-silver) and Reserve Gold mark silver and gold holdings.
- **Minted metals** (disc-copper, disc-gold, disc-silver, disc-crypto, each with an `-edge` partner): Moneta's currency discs and unit chips. Fiat takes copper, gold takes gold, silver takes silver, crypto takes steel; the edge is the darker partner of the same metal.

### Neutral
- **Cool Stock** (cool-stock): page ground. **Label Card** (label-card): the result column and the autocomplete popover.
- **Cabinet Ink** (cabinet-ink) for text, **Slate Muted** (slate-muted) for operators, units, labels and inactive results.
- **Hairline** (hairline) for every divider and chip outline, **Hairline Strong** (hairline-strong) for rail ticks, scrollbar, popover border and ghost-button dashes.
- **Felt Ink / Felt Muted** (felt-ink, felt-muted): text on the rail; rail fields use felt ink at 6% and rail lines at 16%.
- **Gain Green / Loss Red** (gain-green, loss-red): 30-day change, costs in the breakdown, errors.

### Theme anchors
- **Tabula:** board black ground, board ink text, amber for every figure, currency word and chip and the accent, sell-red for the sell option and costs, white digits inside the editor, a lifted board black (tabula-pop) for the popover.
- **Spectra:** charcoal ground under a fixed vertical banding gradient (spectra-band-1 to 5), bone text, emission blue for chart, sources, chips, focus and selection, sodium yellow for the accent and median, a lifted charcoal (spectra-pop) for the popover.
- **Tessera:** navy rail, white stock, carbon purple for figures, chart and chips; currency words sit on a pale carbon chip (gold on a pale gold chip with gold-chip ink); unit chips use the paler chip fill and a violet edge. Stamp red is the accent with a deeper stamp ink for text.
- **Pura:** pale canvas, white rail and result with hairline borders, blue accent with a deeper blue ink for text; currency words and unit chips are blue ink on pale blue fills.

### Named Rules
**The Reserved Accent Rule.** Each theme's accent marks only the active line, the selected option, the net and the median. Own rates (rail line, source row, count badge, checkbox) use ink or rail ink, so they never compete with the doubled median. Inactive lines stay muted so the active one owns the result column. Tabula is the declared exception: amber is the board's numeral color, so every figure, currency word and chip is amber and inactive lines drop to 72% opacity instead.

**The Ink Split Rule.** An accent that fails as text gets a separate `--accent-ink` (Moneta gold to gold ink, Tessera red to stamp ink, Pura blue to blue ink). Never set body-size text in the fill accent on a light ground.

**The Same Metal Rule.** A Moneta currency word in the editor and its unit chip above it wear the same metal pair. A new unit gets a fill and a darker edge from one metal, never a new hue.

## Typography

**Moneta:** Hanken Grotesk. **Tabula:** Martian Mono (editor 15px, 14px on mobile, body 13.5px). **Spectra:** Chivo. **Tessera:** Archivo. **Pura:** the platform UI stack. All are self-hosted variable woff2 except Pura's system stack.

**Character:** one family per theme, hierarchy by size, weight and ink tone only. Numbers are always tabular.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, clamp 2rem to 2.75rem, 1.12, -0.028em): the active line's value in the result column. Tabula uses its own display step (500, clamp 1.7rem to 2.3rem, -0.02em). Spectra: 400 at -0.035em.
- **Headline** (600, 22px, -0.015em): the single page heading; 19px on mobile. Tabula 17px caps; Tessera 18px 700 caps at 0.07em.
- **Empty heading** (600, 20px): the result column's empty-state heading.
- **Net** (700, 18px): the net figure ending the breakdown; the result value's error message also sits at 18px (500).
- **Editor** (500, 17px on a 32px line): expression lines; per-line results at 15px. 16px expressions and 14px results on mobile.
- **Brand** (600, 16px, -0.01em): the wordmark beside the coin mark.
- **Body** (400, 15px, 1.5): default; help prose capped at 64ch. Spectra's enlarged median scale label also sits at 15px.
- **Control** (14px): select values, fee inputs, segment titles (600), breakdown rows, autocomplete options.
- **Meta** (400, 13.5px): expression echo, result label and sub line, legend, source list, help.
- **Title** (600, 13px): block heads in the result column. **Chip** (500, 13px): unit chips, the more-units picker, add buttons, own-rate selects.
- **Label** (400, 12.5px): rail field labels, status, block meta, autocomplete names. Tabula and Tessera set labels and block heads in caps at 11.5px with 0.06 to 0.07em tracking.
- **Small** (400, 12px): rail notes, unit suffixes, source times, chart axis, own-rate arrows. Tessera unit chips are 12px 600 caps at 0.05em.
- **Caption** (400, 11.5px): segmented option sublines, source rail tags and scale.
- **Badge** (600, 11px): the own-rates count in a neutral rail pill.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Ledger Rule.** Every figure on the page uses tabular numerals so columns of results align by digit.

## Layout

Desktop is three fixed columns: a 276px sticky rail, a fluid editor (36px top, 48px sides), a 420px sticky result column with its own scroll. At 1180px the result column drops below the editor (max 640px content). At 760px the rail becomes an off-canvas sheet (min(88vw, 340px)) behind a sticky top bar with a scrim, and both editor and result use a 16px gutter. A wrapping row of unit chips (6px gap, 14px below) sits above the editor; on mobile it becomes a single edge-to-edge scrolling strip with 34px chips. The editor is a two-column grid: expression on the left, results right-aligned in a minimum 7.5rem column (6rem on mobile), with the total row directly under the last line. The autocomplete popover opens 4px below the caret line, 220px to min(340px, 100%) wide. Result blocks stack at 30px intervals. The own-rates panel closes the rail's settings above the footer, separated by a rail hairline; each rate is a five-track row (checkbox, from, arrow, to, remove) over a full-width rate input. Logical properties throughout; the editor and source rail stay LTR inside RTL layouts, and chevrons and arrows mirror.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth comes from tonal grounds only: the rail is the darkest plane, the result column sits on its own surface separated by a hairline. The active line is marked by a translucent accent band (`--band`) that slides between lines, not by a raise. Spectra adds a fixed vertical banding gradient on the page ground. The one lifted surface is the autocomplete popover, which floats over the editor text and so carries a soft two-layer ambient shadow; on the dark themes it also steps up to a lighter `--pop` ground. Otherwise the only shadows are 1px inset or 2px spread halos on currency tokens, and a 1px inset rule above the total row.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Popover** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 28px rgba(10, 14, 18, .16), 0 2px 6px rgba(10, 14, 18, .08)`): autocomplete only.
- **Disc edge** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px <metal edge>`): Moneta currency discs.
- **Chip halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px <chip fill>`): Tessera and Pura currency chips.
- **Total rule** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 var(--line)`): above the editor total row.

### Named Rules
**The No-Card Rule.** No container in the page flow gets a shadow or a card treatment. Separate with 1px hairlines; Tessera's breakdown coupon is the single bordered box, and it is bordered, not lifted. Only a transient overlay that floats above content (the autocomplete popover) may lift.

## Shapes

Gently squared corners on a per-theme radius token: Moneta 6px, Tabula 3px, Tessera 2px, Spectra inherits 6px, Pura 8px. The autocomplete popover rounds 2px beyond the theme radius, its options at the theme radius. The active-line band is 4px. Currency words and unit chips are full pills (999px) in Moneta, Spectra and Pura's chips; Tabula and Tessera chips square off to the theme radius, Tessera currency chips to 2px and Pura currency chips to 4px. Composition segments are 1px (0 in Tabula), legend keys are circles, the own-rates badge is a pill. Every line is 1px; Tabula dashes its breakdown rules, Tessera dashes its perforation at 2px, add buttons are dashed at 1px, and the own-rate tick on the source rail is dashed 3/2.

## Components

### Buttons (segmented direction control)
- **Shape:** two equal options, 6px gap, the theme radius.
- **Default:** rail field fill, rail hairline, a 14px 600 title over an 11.5px muted subline.
- **Active:** accent fill and border, on-accent text, subline at 78% opacity. Tabula's active sell option fills with sell red.
- **Hover:** border lifts to rail muted. Focus: 2px focus-color outline at 2px offset (global).

### Icon buttons
36px square (28px with a 16px glyph inside the own-rates list), transparent border, theme radius, 20px inline SVG. Hover shows a hairline border and the band fill; in the rail it shows the rail line and rail field. The refresh icon spins at 0.9s while loading.

### Add buttons
Full-width rail control with a 1px dashed rail line, theme radius, a 16px plus glyph and 13px text. Hover firms the dash to rail muted over the rail field. The ghost variant sits in the result column at content width: dashed hairline strong, ink text; hover takes the band fill and an accent dash.

### Chips (quick units)
- **Style:** 30px pills (34px on mobile), 1px hairline outline, transparent fill, 13px 500 ink, 0 12px padding. Hover strengthens the outline and adds the band fill; press nudges down 1px.
- **Per theme:** Moneta fills each chip with its unit's metal pair (fill plus edge-colored border), hover dims 3%. Tabula: amber text at the theme radius. Spectra: emission-blue text, gold for gold. Tessera: 12px 600 caps at 0.05em, carbon text on a pale chip fill with a violet edge, theme radius. Pura: blue ink on a pale blue fill, no border.
- **More units:** the last chip holds a native select, muted, with a drawn 5px chevron; it never takes a theme fill.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** rail select and fee inputs share the rail field fill, 1px rail line and theme radius. Selects carry a drawn chevron; fee inputs carry a muted unit suffix (or a leading pair label in own-rate rows). Own-rate selects compact to 7px 8px at 13px.
- **Focus:** fee input border turns accent (focus-within); selects use the global outline. Checkboxes take the accent via `accent-color` at 16px.

### Autocomplete popover
A listbox below the caret line: label card ground (`--pop` on dark themes), hairline-strong border, 4px inner padding, the popover shadow. Options are 7px 10px rows: a 600 code on the left, a 12.5px muted name on the right, truncated. The selected option takes the band fill and its code turns accent ink.

### Own rates
A disclosure at the foot of the rail settings: a 12.5px muted summary with a neutral count pill (rail field, rail line, rail ink) and a rotating chevron (0.2s ease-out-expo). Each rate is a checkbox, from and to selects around a drawn arrow icon, a remove icon button, and a rate input below. A user's own rate appears on the source rail as a dashed ink tick and in the source list as an ink 600 row.

### Line editor (signature)
A transparent textarea over a highlighted mirror. Operators, units and labels are muted, variables take `--var`, unparseable tokens get a wavy loss-red underline. Results are right-aligned and muted; the active line is accent-ink 600, errors loss red, pending lines at 50%. The active band eases with `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)` over 0.2s.

### Currency tokens (per theme)
Moneta: flat minted pills in copper, gold, silver or crypto-steel tones with a darker 1px inset edge. Tabula: amber word, white digits. Spectra: emission blue, gold for gold. Tessera: carbon on a pale carbon chip with a 2px halo, gold-chip ink on a pale gold chip for gold. Pura: blue ink on a pale blue chip.

### Result column
Expression echo, label, display value (a 0.38s rise-and-unblur on change), sub line, then blocks: composition strip (10px segmented bar, negative parts hatched; 3px in Spectra) with legend; fee breakdown as a hairline definition list, costs in loss red, ending in the net in accent ink at 18px 700; source rail (sources as ink ticks, median doubled in accent, own rate dashed in ink) with a source list; 30-day line chart (1.75px stroke, 8% area fill, hover guide).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** add a theme only as a token block on `[data-theme]` plus at most one signature move; never fork the shell.
- **Do** give every theme the full role set, including a text-safe `--accent-ink`, and a `--pop` ground when the theme is dark.
- **Do** separate with 1px hairlines and tonal grounds.
- **Do** keep all figures tabular and right-aligned in the editor's result column.
- **Do** give every new unit chip and currency token the theme's existing treatment (in Moneta, one metal pair).
- **Do** honor reduced motion: every animation and transition is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put containers in cards or lift them with shadows; only a floating overlay such as the autocomplete popover lifts.
- **Don't** spend the accent on anything but the active line, selected option, net and median (Tabula's amber numerals excepted).
- **Don't** emboss or bevel the Moneta discs or chips; they stay flat metal tones with a hairline edge.
- **Don't** replace the line editor with a two-field converter or flag dropdowns.
